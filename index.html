About SharpVE Gurukull

Mentorship & Discipline in Jaipur Since 2015

Established in 2015 in Jaipur, SharpVE Gurukull provides focused coaching for students across Classes 6th to 12th (CBSE & RBSE). We focus on conceptual fundamentals, structured study habits, and systematic board preparation.

Limited Batch Strength

We maintain limited batch strength to encourage individual attention, better interaction and effective learning.

Subject Guidance

Faculty for Accounts, Economics, PCM and School Basics.

Systematic Practice

Chapter-wise revisions and question paper workouts.

Classroom Focus

  • Concept Over Rote: Clear foundational doubts before solving advanced exam problems.
  • Regular Parent Interaction: Keeping parents informed regarding consistency and attendance.
  • Central Jaipur Campus: Conveniently located at Mahesh Nagar, near Gopalpura Bypass.
